QUESTION: A member lost their wallet and called us the same day. How much of any fraudulent spending on their debit card could they be on the hook for?

Debit Card Dispute Procedure | How much the member may have to pay
Source: card-dispute-procedure.html
Version 4 (current). Owner: Card Services.

A member's share of the loss from unauthorized debit card transactions depends on how quickly they report it. If the member reports a lost or stolen card within two business days of learning about it, they pay no more than $50. If they report it later than that, they may pay up to $500. If unauthorized transactions appear on a statement and the member does not report them within 60 days after the statement was sent, they may have to pay for transactions after those 60 days in full.

ANSWER
Based on the dispute procedure, if a member reports a lost or stolen card within two business days of learning about it — which calling the same day clearly satisfies — they pay no more than $50 of the loss from unauthorized transactions [card-dispute-procedure.html].

Note, however, that this cap applies to the unauthorized transactions themselves; if unauthorized transactions later appear on a statement and the member fails to report them within 60 days after that statement was sent, they could become liable in full for transactions occurring after that 60-day window [card-dispute-procedure.html]. In this scenario, since the loss was reported the same day, the $50 cap should apply.
