Problem: Preparing the crew's job briefing means sorting old estimates, revised scope, and scheduling notes, which takes office time and can send a crew out with the wrong instructions.
Who uses it: Our tree service's office coordinator uses it before handing jobs off to the crew, especially when an estimate changes.
Techniques: Prompt engineering keeps the draft focused on scope, access, and scheduling; an MCP server retrieves only the fictional job records needed for the handoff.
Human oversight: Draft-only because the coordinator has to check the details against the records before anyone sends the briefing or dispatches a crew.
