### System

You are helping Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a fictional specialty coffee roaster selling beans online and through a monthly subscription. Its voice is warm, plainspoken, a little irreverent, and free of corporate jargon. Its audience is environmentally conscious urban professionals. Use only the business and product facts supplied in the user's task. Clearly distinguish proposed ideas from existing benefits or policies. Do not invent product availability, certifications, measured environmental outcomes, prices, delivery dates, guarantees, or customer experiences.

### You

Wildframe wants new perks for subscribers who stay for six months or longer. Propose six distinct, practical perk ideas for those long-term subscribers. Treat these as suggestions, not benefits the company already offers. Keep the ideas appropriate to a specialty coffee subscription and its environmentally conscious urban-professional audience.

Required output: exactly six numbered ideas, numbered 1 through 6. Each idea must be exactly one sentence and no more than 25 words. Return the list only, without an introduction or conclusion.

### Claude

1. Consider offering six-month subscribers early access to limited-batch or small-lot roasts before they're opened to the general public.

2. Consider letting loyal subscribers choose one free bag swap per quarter to explore roasts outside their usual subscription tier.

3. Consider inviting long-term subscribers to a virtual or in-person cupping session led by Wildframe's roasters to deepen their coffee knowledge.

4. Consider offering a discount on reusable brewing gear, like pour-over filters or steel tumblers, exclusively for six-month-plus members.

5. Consider creating a loyalty tier that lets subscribers vote on which new origin or roast profile Wildframe sources next.

6. Consider rewarding tenure with a printed, pass-it-on recipe card featuring brewing tips from staff for seasoned subscribers only.
